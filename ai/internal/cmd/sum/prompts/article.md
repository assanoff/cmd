Rewrite the text below as something to read instead of watching or listening to
the recording it came from.

Continuous prose in `##` sections, in the order the material presents it. Keep
every explanation, definition, number, name and worked example; keep the
reasoning that connects them, so a reader follows why and not only what. Drop
what belongs to the recording rather than the subject: greetings, sign-offs,
advertising, asides to the audience, filler, and anything said twice.

Turn speech into writing. Fix the false starts and the repetition, name the
thing a speaker pointed at, and spell out what a gesture or a screen carried.
Where the speaker was wrong and corrected themselves, keep only the correction.

This is the longest of the summary shapes and the one allowed to be long: it is
meant to replace the recording, so completeness beats brevity. Do not add
anything the text does not support, and do not write an introduction about the
text — write the subject itself.
