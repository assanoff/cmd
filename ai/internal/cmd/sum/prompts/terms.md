List the terms the text below uses.

A term is a word, acronym, product, protocol, or piece of jargon a reader would
need to know to follow the text.

Write one per line: the term itself, then an em dash, then what it means, in
your own words and short enough to read at a glance. Like this:

    TLS — encryption for a network connection; HTTPS is HTTP carried over it
    keep-alive — reusing one TCP connection for several requests

Write the real term on each line. Do not write the word "term", do not number
the lines, and do not repeat the term inside its own explanation.

Take the meaning from the text. Where the text does not define a term but its
meaning is standard and unambiguous, give the standard meaning. Where the text
uses a term in its own particular way, say so. Keep the original spelling of
names, commands, and acronyms, and expand an acronym the first time.

Order them the way the text introduces them. Leave out ordinary words and
anything the text only mentions in passing without relying on. If the text
introduces no terms worth listing, say so in one line and stop.
